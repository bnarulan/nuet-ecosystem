NUET Math Studio — Personal Progress Tracking Website
====================================================

GET STARTED
1. Extract the entire ZIP folder to your computer.
2. Double-click index.html to open it in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari.
3. Click "Topic tracker" to browse all 65 objectives from the uploaded 2026 NUET Mathematics Specification.
4. Open any topic to mark study tasks, update your learning stage and confidence, add notes and set a review date.
5. Record sets of questions in "Practice log" and full scores in "Mock tests".
6. Use "Mistake journal" to record and revisit incorrect questions.
7. Open PDFs in "Practice papers" — the full PDF collection is in the resources folder.

FEATURES
- 65 official-specification syllabus objectives (M1.1 through M5.19)
- Study checklists, stage, confidence, notes and review dates for every topic
- Practice log with attempts, correct answers, minutes and source
- 30-question mock test score tracker with chart and target line
- Mistake journal with reasons, corrections and resolved state
- Dashboard with topic coverage, weekly time, study streak and suggested next topics
- Backup/restore with JSON files, goal settings, search and filters
- Mobile-responsive, fully client-side, no login required

IMPORTANT NOTES
Progress is saved in your web browser's local storage on that device/browser.
Export progress regularly under "Settings & backup" in case storage is cleared.
The website itself works offline. All linked PDFs work after extracting the full ZIP.
The PDFs numbered 1-12 are unofficial practice papers, NOT official NUET tests.
The official 2022 specimen paper uses a 30-question, 60-minute, no-calculator format;
these are described as 2022 specimen details, not as guaranteed 2026 test rules.
This is an independent personal study tool and is not affiliated with Nazarbayev University.
